How StructAlert works

Four steps between a photograph and a prevented failure.

  1. 1

    Snap & upload

    Anyone photographs a crack, spall, or leaning structure. Photos arrive geotagged and timestamped from the crowd.

  2. 2

    AI condition score

    Computer vision identifies defects and scores the structure 0–100 against inspection benchmarks.

  3. 3

    Failure prediction

    Deterioration trajectories, typhoon seasons, and loading context estimate when — not if — it fails.

  4. 4

    Smart alerts

    Owners, engineers, and authorities receive prioritized alerts with recommended actions — before failure.

Why now

Three pressures that make waiting for the next scheduled inspection too slow.

Ageing stock, finite inspectors

A city's buildings age faster than surveyors can cover them. Crowdsourcing turns every resident's phone into a survey instrument.

Typhoon season sharpens the stakes

Each storm stresses façades, glazing, and cantilevers. StructAlert keeps watch between inspections — and right after them.

Repair before it breaks

Early intervention costs a fraction of emergency repair — and averts injury, closure, and loss.
